# Photo book editor prototype (design hand-off)

From Priya (Product Design), after the November user test.

This is the editor people tested. They loved drag-and-drop from the photo tray onto the page, auto-fill, and switching layouts without losing their photos. They found the price box useful and kept checking it as they added pages.

What I need from engineering:

- Keep the interaction model: photo tray on the left, page canvas in the middle, pages and settings on the right.
- Keep drag-and-drop, auto-fill, layout switching, captions, themes, undo/redo, and page reordering.
- Make it work on Safari and on a tablet.
- The price has to match what we actually charge.

I know the code is rough. Treat it as the spec, not the starting point.
